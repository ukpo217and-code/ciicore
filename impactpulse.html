ImpactPulse · powered by IVPI

Turn development programmes into continuously verified progress.

ImpactPulse gives NGOs, CSOs, development partners and implementing organisations a persistent engagement and evidence layer, connecting participants before, during and after a programme.

The problem

A programme can end. Progress shouldn't.

Engagement often clusters around baseline and endline; between and after those points the participant relationship goes thin.

Conventional programme

Baseline → Programme activities → Endline survey → Report → Participant relationship often ends

With ImpactPulse

  • Participant onboarding, needs & baseline
  • Programme activities
  • Incentivised actions
  • Evidence & verified progression
  • Follow-up & persistent participant record

What it enables

One continuous layer around the programme.

01 · Engage

Onboard participants and maintain an active relationship throughout the programme.

02 · Incentivise

Turn desired programme behaviours into structured challenges and incentives.

03 · Verify

Collect evidence as activities happen, not only through retrospective reporting.

04 · Track

Maintain participant progression and programme activity over time.

Challenge Builder

Turn programme objectives into actionable challenges.

Define what needs to be done, what evidence is required, and how progression is verified — per programme.

This builder is live on the page — it renders challenges into the list on the right as you create them. It does not yet save anywhere: nothing here is written to a database, and the list resets on reload.

Why it matters

From objective to observable progression.

"Improve agricultural practices" becomes: complete training → apply the practice → submit evidence → receive verification → complete the next milestone.

Example pathway · 12-week livelihood programme

Week 1 onboarding · Week 2 baseline · Week 3 training · Week 5 evidence · Week 6 verification · Week 7 milestone · Week 12 programme milestone · After: follow-up, new opportunity, continued engagement.

  • Challenge 1 · not started
  • Challenge 2 · in progress
  • Challenge 3 · evidence submitted
  • Challenge 4 · verified
  • Milestone reached · next opportunity open

Persistent participant memory

The participant record doesn't disappear at endline.

Profile, needs, baseline, activities, evidence, milestones, progression and follow-up — with participant consent. The programme may end; the relationship can continue.

  • WhatsApp as one possible, low-friction access channel — not the whole product
  • Don't just ask participants to respond: give them reasons to act
  • Incentives: recognition, access, opportunity, rewards, progression — not only cash

Evidence becomes part of the journey

Action → Evidence → Verification → Milestone → Progress. Evidence can include photos, documents, responses, activity records, location/context and completion records. We make no claims about automated verification accuracy.

Programme intelligence

From programme reporting to programme intelligence.

Traditional reporting

How many people participated?

ImpactPulse

Who participated, what did they do, where and when, what evidence exists, who is progressing, who needs follow-up, what happened after the programme.

Illustrative example: a 12-week agricultural livelihoods programme — before (onboarding, baseline), during (training, challenges, evidence, verification), after (follow-up, continued engagement, progression record). No fabricated figures are shown.
