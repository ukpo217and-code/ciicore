Secure provisioning console

Authenticate as an authorised organisation member, configure a programme, and submit it to the existing IVPI provisioning function.

Security: This page uses your Supabase publishable/anon key and your own sign-in session. Never enter or paste a service-role key here. Provisioning is still rejected by the backend unless your signed-in account is linked to an authorised organisation membership.
